1. WHAT TO SAY TO MADAM IN 60 SECONDS (OPENING SPEECH):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Good morning, Madam. Today, I am presenting our project: the Smart Classroom
and Student Engagement AI Suite.

In traditional classrooms with 50-60 students, teachers face two major challenges:
1. Taking attendance manually takes away 10-15 minutes of every lecture.
2. It is impossible for one teacher to observe if students are paying attention,
   feeling sleepy, or using their phones under the desk.
3. Teachers usually only find out a student is failing after the final exam.

Our project solves this by creating an AI Teaching Assistant that combines:
- Computer Vision: Automatically takes attendance and tracks engagement in real-time.
- Predictive Machine Learning: Flags students who are at risk of failing weeks early.
- NLP Sentiment Analysis: Reads student feedback to check if the lecture was understood.
- Web Dashboard: Brings everything into one simple, modern screen."


2. THE 4 CORE MODULES (EXPLAIN THESE IF ASKED FOR DETAILS):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 1: Computer Vision (The "Eyes" of the classroom)
- Technology: OpenCV, PyTorch, and YOLOv8 trained on 8,800+ Kaggle classroom images.
- What it detects:
  * Good signs: Hand raising, reading, writing notes, looking forward.
  * Warning signs: Sleeping/drowsiness, mobile phone usage, looking away.
- Result: Automatic headcount and real-time engagement score.

MODULE 2: Academic Predictor (The "Brain")
- Technology: Scikit-Learn (Decision Tree Classifier + Linear Regression).
- How it works: Teacher inputs weekly study hours, attendance %, and assignments.
- Output: Predicts estimated marks (e.g. 84.5%) and risk level (Low, Moderate, High).
- Value: Helps teachers organize remedial sessions before it is too late.

MODULE 3: Student Feedback Sentiment Engine (The "Ears")
- Technology: Natural Language Processing (TextBlob & NLTK).
- How it works: Students type anonymous feedback about the lecture.
- Output: AI measures sentiment polarity (Positive, Neutral, Negative) and subjectivity.
- Value: Helps teachers immediately adjust their teaching pace.

MODULE 4: Central Web Dashboard & SQLite Database
- Technology: FastAPI backend + HTML5/CSS/JavaScript + SQLite database.
- Features: 60+ verified student records, search and filter by risk level, and live charts.

4. PROFESSOR / MADAM QUESTIONS & WINNING ANSWERS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: "Is this replacing the teacher?"
A: "No Madam, it is an assistant designed to empower teachers by removing repetitive
    chores (like calling roll numbers) and giving early warning insights."

Q: "What algorithms did you use?"
A: "YOLOv8 for object and behavior detection, Decision Trees for pass/fail classification,
    Linear Regression for score estimation, and TextBlob for NLP sentiment analysis."

Q: "What dataset was used?"
A: "We used the Kaggle Classroom Dataset containing over 8,800 images across 8 labeled
    behavior classes, alongside student academic records."

Q: "What about student privacy?"
A: "It processes video locally without uploading to the cloud, and aggregates data
    into overall classroom engagement percentages rather than punitive tracking."
